Nova Patrol is a space shooter game. It needs a browser with canvas support.

Nova Patrol

Alien ships are coming in waves. Blast them, dodge their fire, and beat the boss every 5th wave. Your ship fires on its own.

  • Move: arrow keys or WASD, or drag on the screen
  • Bomb: B or X key, or the 💣 button
  • Pause: P or Esc
  • P More guns
  • R Rapid fire
  • S Shield
  • B Extra bomb
  • ♥ Extra life

Chain kills quickly for a score multiplier!
